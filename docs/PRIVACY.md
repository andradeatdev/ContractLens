# Privacy and Data Handling Policy

## Introduction
At Contract Lens, we take your privacy and the confidentiality of your legal documents seriously. This policy outlines how we handle, process, and protect your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **Contract Data:** When you upload a PDF, we extract the text to provide analysis. The original PDF is stored securely to allow you to download it later.
- **User Metadata:** We store your name, email, and preferences to manage your account and provide a personalized experience.
- **Analysis Data:** The summaries, risk assessments, and chat history generated by our AI are stored to provide you with a continuous workflow.

## 2. AI Processing (Google Gemini)
Contract Lens utilizes **Google Gemini Flash 2.5 Lite** for document analysis and chat.
- **Privacy Commitment:** We use the Google AI Enterprise platform. According to Google's terms for enterprise/paid tiers, **your data is NOT used to train their base models.**
- **Transient Processing:** Data is sent to the AI service only during the analysis or chat phase.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Encryption in Transit:** All data is sent over HTTPS using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- **Encryption at Rest:** All sensitive data, including contract text and summaries, is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
- **Storage:** We use Vercel Blob for file storage, which provides high availability and security.

## 4. Data Minimization and Retention
- We only store what is necessary for the application to function.
- **Right to Erasure:** You can delete your contracts or your entire account at any time. When you delete a contract, all associated text, analysis, and files are permanently removed from our active databases.

## 5. Third-Party Services
- **Google Cloud/AI:** For AI processing.
- **Vercel:** For hosting and storage.
- **PostgreSQL:** For our primary database.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR/LGPD)
You have the right to:
- Access your data.
- Correct any inaccuracies.
- Request deletion (Right to be Forgotten).
- Export your data in a machine-readable format.
